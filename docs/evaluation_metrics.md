# Evaluation Metrics

NATURALDUB-AI evaluates 19 different dimensions of dubbing quality:
1. Translation Quality
2. Voice Similarity
3. Emotion Matching
4. Intonation and Expression
5. Speech Speed
6. Lip Synchronization
7. Speaker Identification
8. Pronunciation
9. Voice Depth and Tone
10. Background Music and Sound Effects
11. Audio Quality
12. Natural Pauses and Breathing
13. Scene Context
14. Character Consistency
15. Humor Preservation
16. Cultural Adaptation Quality
17. Genre Consistency
18. Punchline Timing
19. Dialogue Intent Preservation

Each metric receives a score, culminating in an `overall_score`.
